Efficient Generative Models

高效生成模型：在推理深度、算力、延迟、内存、数据之间做权衡，重点是 LLM。

3 学分 2026 秋 Letter or Credit/No Credit ★★★★☆
生成模型效率LLM系统

课程定位

MS&E 319 研究生成模型在计算约束下的算法问题。课程讨论目标、架构、数据 pipeline、推理过程和服务系统如何被算力/延迟/内存/数据塑造，并以 LLM 为主要案例；学生做一次 presentation，再完成 survey 或研究项目。

主要内容

  1. 计算约束：推理深度、算力、延迟、内存、数据
  2. 目标与架构：scaling、稀疏、混合专家
  3. 数据 pipeline：质量、配比、合成数据
  4. 推理：采样、speculative decoding、test-time compute
  5. Serving：批处理、缓存、调度
  6. 算法问题：约束下的新问题
  7. presentation + survey/project

官方课程描述（英文原文）

As generative models become central to computing, their progress increasingly depends on algorithms that trade reasoning depth, computational cost, latency, memory, and data. This course studies those tradeoffs with a primary focus on modern language models. We examine how objectives, architectures, data pipelines, inference procedures, and serving systems are shaped by computational constraints, and how these constraints open new algorithmic questions. Students give one presentation and complete either a survey or a research project. May be repeated for credit.
