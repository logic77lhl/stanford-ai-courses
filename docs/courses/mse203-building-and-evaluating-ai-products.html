构建与评估 AI 产品

Building and Evaluating AI Products

从 0 到 1 做 AI 产品和 agent：AI fit、延迟/质量/成本、eval、Demo Day。

3 学分 2026 秋 Letter (ABCD/NP) ★★★☆☆
产品AgentEval创业

课程定位

MS&E 203 是一门项目制课程，学生在一个学期内真正做出一个 AI 产品。课程要求论证“为什么需要 AI”、处理延迟/质量/成本 trade-off、严格评估自己的系统，最后在 Demo Day 展示。选课前有自学的 git、AI 编程工具、eval 框架和 API 模块。

主要内容

  1. AI fit：什么问题适合用 AI/agent
  2. 产品定义：用户、场景、价值
  3. 延迟/质量/成本：工程 trade-off
  4. Eval：离线/在线指标、LLM-as-judge
  5. 团队 build：从个人机制到团队产品
  6. Demo Day：展示与评审

官方课程描述（英文原文）

A project-based course on building AI products and agents end-to-end. Students build a real product over the quarter, justifying AI fit, navigating latency, quality, and cost trade-offs, and rigorously evaluating their own system. The course moves from an individual mechanics phase into a team build, ending in a Demo Day. Enrolled students complete a self-paced pre-course module covering git basics, AI coding tool fluency (Cursor, Claude Code, or Antigravity), eval framework basics, and API setup. Designed primarily for upper-division undergraduates and master's students, with target majors including MS&amp;E, CS, Symbolic Systems, and EE; open to all Stanford students. Enrollment is capped at 12 teams of 4 students (48 students total).
