Course positioning

CS 277 discusses how generative AI and self-supervised foundation models are changing healthcare. It covers NLP, CV, and multimodal models, both adapting general models to healthcare and building healthcare-specific FMs, with attention to evaluation, deployment, and real clinical needs.

What it covers

  1. Self-supervised FMs: pre-training, representation learning
  2. Clinical NLP: clinical text, QA, summarization
  3. Medical imaging: classification, segmentation, report generation
  4. Multimodality: imaging + text + omics
  5. Evaluation and deployment: clinical metrics, regulation, bias
  6. Ethics and privacy
  7. Project

Prerequisites

CS 229/231N/224N-level ML background.

Assessment & workload

Assignments plus a project/paper discussion. Check the current syllabus for details.

Who it's for

Medical AI, clinical data science, and medical imaging; engineers/researchers entering healthcare FMs.

Study advice

Take CS 231N or CS 224N first. Pay attention to privacy and regulatory constraints on clinical data.

Official course description

Generative AI and large-scale self-supervised foundation models are poised to have a profound impact on human decision making across occupations. Healthcare is one such area where such models have the capacity to impact patients, clinicians, and other care providers. In this course, we will explore the training, evaluation, and deployment of generative AI and foundation models, with a focus on addressing current and future medical needs. The course will cover models used in natural language processing, computer vision, and multi-modal applications. We will explore the intersection of models trained on non-healthcare domains and their adaptation to domain-specific problems, as well as healthcare-specific foundation models. Prerequisites: Familiarity with machine learning principles at the level of CS 229, 231N, or 224N
