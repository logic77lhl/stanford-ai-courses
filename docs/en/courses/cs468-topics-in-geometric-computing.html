Course positioning

CS 468 is a topics course whose content changes each offering; past years covered geometric matching, surface reconstruction, collision detection, computational topology, computer differential geometry, data-driven shape analysis, and non-Euclidean machine learning. Recent offerings shifted to 3D/4D foundation models; it suits graduate students with a geometry/graphics background.

What it covers

  1. Geometric matching: rigid/non-rigid registration
  2. Surface reconstruction: point clouds to meshes, implicit representations
  3. Collision detection: spatial data structures
  4. Computational topology: persistent homology, topological data analysis
  5. Differential geometry: discrete surfaces, curvature
  6. 3D/4D foundation models: multimodal 3D representations, dynamic scenes
  7. Paper reading and project

Prerequisites

Graphics/geometry/vision background; programming and mathematical maturity.

Assessment & workload

Paper discussion plus a project/reproduction. Check the current syllabus for details.

Who it's for

Graduate students in 3D vision, graphics, or geometry processing; people interested in 3D foundation models.

Study advice

Take CS 148/CS 231A first. Topics change every year; read the current syllabus before enrolling.
