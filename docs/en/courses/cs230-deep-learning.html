Course positioning

CS 230 uses a flipped classroom: watch videos and do programming assignments and quizzes before class, then discuss and work on projects in class. It covers the building blocks and engineering methodology of deep learning, with case studies across healthcare, autonomous driving, sign language, music generation, and NLP. It is more hands-on than CS 229.

What it covers

  1. Neural network basics: forward/backward propagation, activations, losses
  2. CNNs: convolution, pooling, classic architectures, transfer learning
  3. Sequence models: RNNs, LSTMs, attention
  4. Training techniques: Adam, dropout, BatchNorm, Xavier/He initialization
  5. ML project methodology: error analysis, bias-variance, data splits, iteration strategy
  6. Industry case studies: medical imaging, autonomous driving, music generation, NLP
  7. Open-ended final project

Prerequisites

Python programming; linear algebra (matrix/vector operations). CS 229 may be taken concurrently.

Assessment & workload

Programming assignments, online quizzes, and an open-ended final project; the flipped classroom requires preparation before class. Check the current syllabus for details.

Who it's for

People who want to get hands-on with deep learning and ship a complete project; product or startup backgrounds that need technical execution; a broader, project-oriented route than CS 231N.

Study advice

If you have never touched DL, start with the NN part of CS 229 or the first few CS 231N lectures. The project separates people more than the assignments do — form a team and pick a topic early.

Official course description

Deep Learning is one of the most highly sought after skills in AI. We will help you become good at Deep Learning. In this course, you will learn the foundations of Deep Learning, understand how to build neural networks, and learn how to lead successful machine learning projects. You will learn about Convolutional networks, RNNs, LSTM, Adam, Dropout, BatchNorm, Xavier/He initialization, and more. You will work on case studies from healthcare, autonomous driving, sign language reading, music generation, and natural language processing. You will master not only the theory, but also see how it is applied in industry. You will practice all these ideas in Python and in TensorFlow, which we will teach. AI is transforming multiple industries. After this course, you will likely find creative ways to apply it to your work. This class is taught in the flipped-classroom format. You will watch videos and complete in-depth programming assignments and online quizzes at home, then come in to class for advanced discussions and work on projects. This class will culminate in an open-ended final project, which the teaching team will help you on. Prerequisites: Familiarity with programming in Python and Linear Algebra (matrix / vector multiplications). CS 229 may be taken concurrently.
