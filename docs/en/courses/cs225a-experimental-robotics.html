Course positioning

CS 225A is Khatib's lab course, emphasizing making robots actually move. It covers robot-arm kinematics, dynamics, control, compliant control, sensor-based collision avoidance, and human interfaces; the second half is an open project.

What it covers

  1. Robot-arm kinematics: forward/inverse solutions, workspace
  2. Dynamics: rigid-body dynamics, parameter identification
  3. Control: PID, computed torque, impedance/compliance
  4. Collision avoidance: sensors, artificial potential fields
  5. Human interfaces: teleoperation, shared control
  6. Open project: drawing, air hockey, table tennis, etc.

Prerequisites

CS 223A or equivalent.

Assessment & workload

Labs/assignments plus a final project demo. Check the current syllabus for details.

Who it's for

People who want hands-on robotics, manipulation, or control; undergraduates/master's students preparing for a robotics lab.

Study advice

Take CS 223A first. When forming teams, cover control, perception, and software.

Official course description

Hands-on laboratory course experience in robotic manipulation. Topics include robot kinematics, dynamics, control, compliance, sensor-based collision avoidance, and human-robot interfaces. Second half of class is devoted to final projects using various robotic platforms to build and demonstrate new robot task capabilities. Previous projects include the development of autonomous robot behaviors of drawing, painting, playing air hocket, yoyo, basketball, ping-pong or xylophone. Prerequisites: 223A or equivalent.
