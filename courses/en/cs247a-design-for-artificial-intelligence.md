## Course positioning

CS 247A (SYMSYS 195A) is the advanced design follow-up to CS 147, run entirely as a studio. It focuses on human-centered AI experiences: what AI is, what constraints and opportunities it has, and how to design responsible, ethical AI systems. The course emphasizes intense iteration and real design problems.

## What it covers

- **Design research**: interviews, observation, defining needs
- **AI capabilities and constraints**: what it can and cannot do
- **Prototyping**: low- to high-fidelity, Wizard-of-Oz
- **Interaction patterns**: conversation, recommendation, automation, mixed-initiative
- **Trust and transparency**: explanations, control, error recovery
- **Ethics and responsibility**: bias, privacy, power
- **Studio project**

## Assessment & workload

Studio attendance plus iteration and a final project. Check the current syllabus for details.

## Who it's for

Designers, product managers, HCI students; AI developers moving from demos to experiences.

## Study advice

This is not an ML technique course. Confirm you can commit studio time before enrolling.

> A project-based course that builds on the introduction to design in CS147 by focusing on advanced methods and tools for research, prototyping, and user interface design. Studio based format with intensive coaching and iteration to prepare students for tackling real world design problems. This course takes place entirely in studios; you must plan on attending every studio to take this class. The focus of CS247A is design for human-centered artificial intelligence experiences. What does it mean to design for AI? What is HAI? How do you create responsible, ethical, human centered experiences? Let us explore what AI actually is and the constraints, opportunities and specialized processes necessary to create AI systems that work effectively for the humans involved.
