## Course positioning

EE 292R (CS 292/PSYCH 292R/STATS 282) is a 1-unit seminar first offered in Autumn 2026, organized by Candès, Wetzstein, Narasimhan, and Wandell. It does not teach specific techniques; each week a speaker presents a real AI4Science case, covering neuroscience, medicine, genomics, computational social science, world models, and more.

## What it covers

- **Neuroscience**: retina/visual system, brain foundation models, digital twins
- **Medicine**: medical imaging FMs, precision medicine, multimodal omics
- **Genomics**: regulatory DNA, non-coding variants
- **Spatial intelligence**: video world models, world agents
- **Computational social science**: coordinated behavior detection across encrypted platforms
- **Research infrastructure**: Marlowe GPU cluster, petabyte-scale data

## Assessment & workload

Attendance plus a short write-up on one lecture topic; 1 unit, S/NC.

## Who it's for

People who want a fast map of AI4Science and to find interdisciplinary topics/collaborators; not for people who want systematic ML technique training.

## Study advice

Lectures are in person only, but Stanford Online will upload recordings to YouTube. Write down one 'method + scientific question' pair per talk.
