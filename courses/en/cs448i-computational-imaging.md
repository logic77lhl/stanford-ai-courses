## Course positioning

CS 448I (EE 367) is taught by Gordon Wetzstein and is a core course in computational photography/imaging. It combines classical image processing, convex optimization, and modern AI, covering denoising, deconvolution, single-pixel imaging, ToF, wave optics, and end-to-end joint optimization of optics and algorithms.

## What it covers

- **Image processing basics**: convolution, filtering, denoising
- **Inverse problems**: deconvolution, regularization, proximal gradient
- **CNNs for imaging**: image restoration, learned priors
- **Single-pixel imaging**: coded apertures, compressed sensing
- **Wave optics**: diffraction, holography, phase retrieval
- **ToF imaging**: depth measurement, multipath
- **End-to-end optimization**: joint optics + algorithm design
- **Project**

## Assessment & workload

Assignments plus a term project, emphasizing implementation. Check the current syllabus for details.

## Who it's for

Computational photography, imaging systems, AR/VR, and microscopy/medical imaging.

## Study advice

Brush up on linear algebra and signals & systems first. Pair with EE 367.
