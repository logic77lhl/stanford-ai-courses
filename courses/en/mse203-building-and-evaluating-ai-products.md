## Course positioning

MS&E 203 is a project-based course where students build a real AI product in one quarter. It requires justifying why AI is needed, handling latency/quality/cost trade-offs, rigorously evaluating the system, and presenting at Demo Day. Before enrolling, students complete self-study modules on git, AI coding tools, eval frameworks, and APIs.

## What it covers

- **AI fit**: what problems suit AI/agents
- **Product definition**: users, scenarios, value
- **Latency/quality/cost**: engineering trade-offs
- **Evaluation**: offline/online metrics, LLM-as-judge
- **Team build**: from individual mechanisms to a team product
- **Demo Day**: presentation and review

## Assessment & workload

Mainly a project plus Demo Day; individual/team assignments may be included. Check the current syllabus for details.

## Who it's for

People who want to start a company or build AI products; MS&E, CS, SymSys, EE backgrounds; anyone who needs a portfolio.

## Study advice

Build a complementary team (engineering + design + business). Don't pick too large a problem; evaluation matters more than the demo.
