## Course positioning

MS&E 319 studies the algorithmic problems of generative models under compute constraints. It discusses how objectives, architectures, data pipelines, inference, and serving systems are shaped by compute/latency/memory/data, using LLMs as the main case; students give one presentation and complete a survey or research project.

## What it covers

- **Compute constraints**: inference depth, compute, latency, memory, data
- **Objectives and architectures**: scaling, sparsity, mixture of experts
- **Data pipelines**: quality, mixing, synthetic data
- **Inference**: sampling, speculative decoding, test-time compute
- **Serving**: batching, caching, scheduling
- **Algorithmic problems**: new problems under constraints
- **Presentation + survey/project**

## Assessment & workload

One presentation plus a survey or research project. Check the current syllabus for details.

## Who it's for

People who want to work on inference efficiency, generative-model algorithms, or ML systems; graduate students.
