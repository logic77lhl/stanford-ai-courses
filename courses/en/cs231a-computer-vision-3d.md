## Course positioning

CS 231A is the classic course on 3D vision and geometry, complementing CS 231N: 231N leans recognition and deep learning, while 231A leans cameras, projection, stereo, SfM, and 3D understanding. It is more mathematical and geometric, and suits people interested in AR/VR, robotics, and autonomous-driving perception.

## What it covers

- **Cameras and projection**: pinhole model, intrinsics/extrinsics, distortion
- **Image processing**: filtering, edges, feature points
- **Segmentation and clustering**: classical and learned methods
- **Stereo vision**: epipolar geometry, depth estimation
- **SfM and SLAM basics**: structure from motion
- **Recognition**: objects, scenes, faces, actions
- **Project**: 3D reconstruction or a vision application

## Assessment & workload

Assignments plus a project, including geometric derivations and implementation. Check the current syllabus for details.

## Who it's for

3D vision, robotics, AR/VR, and autonomous-driving perception; CV students who want geometry foundations.

## Study advice

Take CS 231N first or concurrently. Derive epipolar geometry and bundle adjustment by hand.
