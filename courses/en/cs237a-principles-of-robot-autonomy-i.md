## Course positioning

CS 237A (AA 274A/EE 260A/ME 274A) is taught by Marco Pavone and is the core course on robot autonomy. It connects perception, localization, control, planning, and decision-making under uncertainty, with extensive hands-on ROS work.

## What it covers

- **Perception**: sensors, features, vision/lidar
- **Localization and SLAM**: EKF, particle filters, graph optimization
- **Nonlinear control**: feedback linearization, MPC basics
- **Motion planning**: graph search, sampling-based planning, trajectory optimization
- **Uncertainty**: POMDPs, belief space
- **ROS practice**
- **Project**: make a robot complete a task autonomously

## Assessment & workload

Assignments plus a ROS project. Check the current syllabus for details.

## Who it's for

Robotics, autonomous driving, drones, and embodied AI.

## Study advice

Take CS 223A first or have rigid-body kinematics basics. Get familiar with ROS early.

<summary>Official course description</summary>

> Basic principles for endowing mobile autonomous robots with perception, planning, and decision-making capabilities. Algorithmic approaches for robot perception, localization, and simultaneous localization and mapping; control of non-linear systems, learning-based control, and robot motion planning; introduction to methodologies for reasoning under uncertainty, e.g., (partially observable) Markov decision processes. Extensive use of the Robot Operating System (ROS) for demonstrations and hands-on activities. Prerequisites: CS 106A or equivalent, CME 100 or equivalent (for linear algebra), and CME 106 or equivalent (for probability theory).
