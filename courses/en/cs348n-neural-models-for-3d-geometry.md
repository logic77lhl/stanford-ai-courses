## Course positioning

CS 348N studies how to generate and manipulate high-quality 3D models and scenes with machine learning. It covers geometry representations, 3D datasets, point-cloud/mesh networks, neural implicits, 3D generative models, conditional generation, and evaluation — a core course for 3D generation/graphics.

## What it covers

- **Geometry representations**: point clouds, meshes, voxels, SDFs, NeRF
- **3D data**: ShapeNet, Objaverse, scene datasets
- **Point-cloud/mesh networks**: PointNet, GCN, MeshCNN
- **Neural implicits**: SDFs, occupancy, NeRF
- **3D generation**: VAE/GAN/diffusion/autoregressive
- **Conditional generation**: text/image to 3D
- **Evaluation and synthetic data**

Learn NeRF and diffusion first, then revisit 3D versions of GAN/VAE. Pair with CS 468.
