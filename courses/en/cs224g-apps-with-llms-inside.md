## Course positioning

CS 224G is a project course that treats the LLM as a new CPU and explores how to build applications around it. It runs in two-week sprints with industry guest lectures, and suits people who want to ship a demo quickly.

## What it covers

- **LLM capabilities and limits**: hallucination, context, cost
- **Prompting**: structured prompts, few-shot, role setting
- **Tools and retrieval**: function calling, RAG, external APIs
- **Application architecture**: frontend, backend, streaming, state management
- **Fast iteration**: sprints, user feedback, metrics
- **Project**: build an LLM-native app

## Assessment & workload

Two-week sprint assignments plus a final project and guest lectures. Check the current syllabus for details.

## Who it's for

People who want to build LLM products or startups; product managers, designers, full-stack engineers.

## Study advice

It complements CS 329Z: 224G is product cadence, 329Z is systems and eval. Define the user problem before choosing a model.
