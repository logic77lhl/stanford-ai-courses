## Course positioning

CS 228 is the classic PGM course, covering Bayesian and Markov networks, HMMs, exact and approximate inference, and parameter/structure learning. Its way of encoding uncertainty with graph structure has long-term value for NLP, computational biology, causal inference, and robotics.

## What it covers

- **Representation**: Bayesian networks, Markov networks, factor graphs
- **Exact inference**: variable elimination, belief propagation, junction trees
- **Approximate inference**: variational inference, MCMC, importance sampling
- **Parameter learning**: MLE, EM, latent-variable models
- **Structure learning**: score-based search, constraint-based methods
- **Temporal models**: HMMs, dynamic Bayesian networks
- **Applications**: speech, biology, medical diagnosis, vision, robot planning

## Assessment & workload

Assignments plus a midterm and a project; a mix of math and implementation. Check the current syllabus for details.

## Who it's for

People working on probabilistic modeling, causality, or Bayesian deep learning; researchers in NLP, computational biology, or robotics who need to handle uncertainty.

## Study advice

It complements CS 229: 229 is prediction-oriented, 228 is about structured uncertainty. Derive variable elimination and VI by hand once.

> Probabilistic graphical modeling languages for representing complex domains, algorithms for reasoning using these representations, and learning these representations from data.
