## Course positioning

CS 468 is a topics course whose content changes each offering; past years covered geometric matching, surface reconstruction, collision detection, computational topology, computer differential geometry, data-driven shape analysis, and non-Euclidean machine learning. Recent offerings shifted to 3D/4D foundation models; it suits graduate students with a geometry/graphics background.

## What it covers

- **Geometric matching**: rigid/non-rigid registration
- **Surface reconstruction**: point clouds to meshes, implicit representations
- **Collision detection**: spatial data structures
- **Computational topology**: persistent homology, topological data analysis
- **Differential geometry**: discrete surfaces, curvature
- **3D/4D foundation models**: multimodal 3D representations, dynamic scenes
- **Paper reading and project**

## Assessment & workload

Paper discussion plus a project/reproduction. Check the current syllabus for details.

## Who it's for

Graduate students in 3D vision, graphics, or geometry processing; people interested in 3D foundation models.
