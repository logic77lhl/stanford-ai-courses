## Course positioning

STATS 315A is a graduate-level ML course in the statistics department, covering prediction methods (linear models, trees/forests, deep networks), model evaluation (cross-validation, calibration, conformal prediction), optimization (convexity, stochastic methods), and generative models (language models, diffusion, VAEs). It balances statistical rigor with modern methods.

## What it covers

- **Prediction methods**: linear, trees/forests, deep networks
- **Model evaluation**: CV, calibration, conformal prediction
- **Optimization**: convex optimization, SGD, adaptive methods
- **Generative models**: language models, diffusion, VAEs
- **High dimensions and regularization**: lasso, sparsity
- **Project/paper discussion**

## Assessment & workload

Assignments plus a project/exam. Check the current syllabus for details.

## Who it's for

Statistics/ML graduate students; people who want rigorous predictive modeling and uncertainty quantification.

## Study advice

Take STATS 202 or CS 229 first. The conformal prediction part is worth going deep on.
